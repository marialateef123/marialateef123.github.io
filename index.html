Flagship · Deep Learning + Hardware

Handwriting Emulator System

Final year project · 3rd place, COMSATS Career Expo

An end-to-end system that turns typed or spoken text into realistic human handwriting, physically drawn on paper by a custom-built robotic pen plotter. Fully offline, with six selectable styles. I trained a 1.89M-parameter LSTM-MDN model with Graves-style soft attention on the IAM database, diagnosed and fixed an attention-collapse defect with a kappa-velocity penalty, built a dual-mode OCR pipeline (Tesseract and Microsoft TrOCR) with Whisper voice input, and designed the two-axis plotter hardware and its stroke-to-G-code pipeline.

Generative AI · RAG

Pakistan Case-Law Assistant

A prototype AI research assistant that retrieves relevant Supreme Court of Pakistan judgments for a typed or spoken question, summarises each with an LLM, and labels which side it supports. Built on a RAG pipeline with sentence-transformer embeddings, a Chroma vector database, and Google Gemini for summaries, side-labelling, and voice transcription.
